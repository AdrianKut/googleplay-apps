Privacy Policy for Labyrinth HD

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 96Games handles information in connection with the Labyrinth HD mobile game, including information that may be processed when Google services are used.

1. Developer

Labyrinth HD is developed and published by 96Games. The developer is based in Poland.

2. Data collected directly by 96Games

96Games does not operate a separate user-profile database for Labyrinth HD and does not intentionally collect or sell personal information through its own servers.

Certain features may rely on platform services or third-party SDKs. Those services may process information as described below and under their own privacy policies.

3. Google services and Google user data

Labyrinth HD may use Google authentication and Google Play platform services for Google-connected game features. When a user chooses to use such a feature, Google may provide the application with the minimum account information needed for authentication or the requested feature, such as a Google account identifier and basic profile information where permitted by the user. 96Games uses that information only for the requested Google-connected functionality.

  • Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing feature for which access was requested.
  • Google user data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising or ad personalization by 96Games.
  • 96Games does not transfer Google user data to data brokers or unrelated third parties.
  • 96Games does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
Google API Limited Use: The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Game data and platform features

Labyrinth HD includes game progress and time-ranking features. Where a Google Play Games or similar platform leaderboard is used, gameplay results such as completion time, score, ranking, and the user's platform display identity may be processed by the platform provider and shown according to that provider's leaderboard and privacy settings. 96Games does not use Google user data for unrelated purposes.

5. Advertising and third-party services

Labyrinth HD may display advertisements and may use third-party platform services required for game operation, distribution, authentication, diagnostics, or advertising. Depending on the provider and device configuration, those services may process technical information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, diagnostic data, or interactions with ads.

Any such processing is governed by the relevant third-party provider's privacy terms. Google user data obtained through OAuth or Google APIs is not used by 96Games for advertising purposes.

6. Data retention

96Games keeps personal information only when it is necessary to provide a requested feature or to comply with applicable legal obligations. Where a Google-connected feature is handled directly by Google services, related data may be retained by Google according to the user's Google account settings and Google's own policies.

7. Data deletion and revoking Google access

You can revoke access granted to Labyrinth HD through your Google Account permissions at any time. If you believe that 96Games holds information relating to you and you want it removed, contact us at aixen96@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

96Games does not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to 96Games. Where the game is made available to younger users, applicable Google Play and platform requirements are followed for the services enabled in the application.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its connected services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, contact: aixen96@gmail.com.
